AdSeeker has the right to monitor and review all messages exchanged on the platform to ensure compliance, prevent fraud, and maintain a safe environment.

3. No Off-Platform Dealing

Users are strictly prohibited from bypassing the platform to complete deals externally. This includes sharing personal contact details in chat, arranging payments outside AdSeeker, or soliciting direct contact off-platform.

AdSeeker charges a 5% commission fee on all transactions. Any attempt to avoid platform fees may result in immediate account suspension or legal action.

5. Deal Completion

All agreements initiated on AdSeeker must be completed through the platform. Contact details are only revealed after a confirmed payment.

AdSeeker reserves the right to suspend or permanently ban any account found in violation of these terms. Banned users will be notified via email.
